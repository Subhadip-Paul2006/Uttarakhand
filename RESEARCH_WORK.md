# Discover Uttarakhand: Hidden Gems • Popular Destinations • Explorer Trails

**Project Overview**  
This website categorizes Uttarakhand (Devbhoomi – Land of the Gods) into three distinct travel experiences to help users plan according to their preferences:  
- **Hidden Gems**: Serene, offbeat spots with minimal crowds, ideal for peaceful nature immersion and authentic local experiences.  
- **Most Visited Places**: High-footfall destinations with strong infrastructure, popular among families, pilgrims, honeymooners, and weekenders (Uttarakhand recorded 6.03 crore+ visitors in 2025, with Haridwar alone attracting 3.42 crore).  
- **Explorer Spots**: Adventure-focused hiking/trekking destinations requiring fitness, suitable only for those comfortable with trails, altitude, and basic camping.

The site features interactive maps, filters (region: Garhwal/Kumaon, season, difficulty), detailed place pages with photos, itineraries, and sustainable tourism tips.

## Research Methodology & Notes
Data compiled from official sources (Uttarakhand Tourism Dept. 2025 statistics), trek organizers (e.g., Indiahikes, Trek The Himalayas), traveler platforms, and field insights. Expenses are approximate for 2026 (mid-range homestays/hotels, shared transport, vegetarian meals; excludes luxury or international flights). Always verify current road conditions, permits (e.g., for protected areas), and weather on uttarakhandtourism.gov.in. Best overall seasons: March–June & September–November. Monsoon (July–August) brings landslides; winter offers snow but limits access.

## Best Time to Visit (General Guidance)
- **March–June**: Pleasant for sightseeing and most treks (rhododendron blooms in spring).  
- **September–November**: Clear skies, sharp mountain views post-monsoon.  
- **December–February**: Snow in higher areas (great for Auli skiing or winter treks like Kedarkantha); roads may close.  
- Avoid peak monsoon for landslides. Char Dham Yatra peaks May–October.

## How to Reach & Practical Tips
- **Air**: Dehradun (Jolly Grant), Pantnagar.  
- **Rail**: Haridwar, Dehradun, Kathgodam (Kumaon side).  
- **Road**: Well-connected NH from Delhi (250–550 km depending on destination).  
- **Sustainability**: Follow Leave No Trace; support local homestays; respect wildlife sanctuaries and pilgrimage etiquette. Altitude sickness awareness is crucial for explorer spots.
